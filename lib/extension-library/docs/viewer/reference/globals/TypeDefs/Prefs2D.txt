Type Definitions

# Prefs2D

Contains viewer setting preference names for 2D models.

# Properties

GRAYSCALE (string) | Overrides line colors in 2D models to render in shades of gray.
SWAP_BLACK_AND_WHITE (string) | Will switch to white lines on a black background.
SCREEN_SPACE_LINE_WIDTH (string) | PDF-only: keeps line thickness constant on screen as you zoom (relative widths preserved).
FORCE_PDF_CALIBRATION (string) | Force PDF calibration before measuring.
FORCE_LEAFLET_CALIBRATION (string) | Force Leaflet calibration before measuring.
VECTOR_VIEWING (string) | Vector viewing of PDF.
